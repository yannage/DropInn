## Cost and branch contract

Foreshadowing; visible alternatives; what each changes; when the party chooses; simultaneous resolution/tie/no-input rule; fallback route; behavior with one human, late joins, departures and duplicates. Avoid undocumented shared-resource decisions.

## Mechanics and scope

Existing mechanics used; proposed signature extension and its fallback; conditions that distinguish the extension from flavor text; server/state/art dependencies. Set numeric balance in implementation review, not by quietly overriding existing rules in prose.

## Closing image and keepsake

Give success/mixed/setback closing copy and truthful contribution-recap examples. State what changes in the scene and what remains unresolved. Keepsakes follow standard reward eligibility.

## Art and audio brief

Three backgrounds with four clear interaction zones; unchanged/developed cutouts per target; threat poses if needed; branch-specific closing compositions; restrained effects and reduced-motion equivalents. Refer to the existing art skill before production.

## Verification

Story-specific comprehension questions; branch/flag assertions; fast/slow/failure paths; late arrival; solo/companions; mobile and input alternatives. Record design review separately from runtime and human evidence.

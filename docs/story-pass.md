# First Tales Story Pass

The four First Tales stories remain free. A player can earn pass points before buying by contributing to completed chapters. Finishing each story once earns its first-clear hat (where offered); each first and third finish, chapter milestones at 1/6/12/18, and finishing all four stories award 100 points apiece. Points cap at 1000 across ten cosmetic steps. A player can finish the track in roughly six to eight full runs by trying all four stories and replaying favorites. An ending counts only with a verified contribution in that final chapter; outcome quality does not gate it. Existing verified chapter credits are backfilled.

The $5 standard purchase grants every earned track reward and future earned steps. The $10 Super Supporter purchase additionally grants the first two rewards and a six-color frame immediately, plus earned extra titles at steps 4 and 9 and sparkling Ruby Shoes at step 10. A standard owner can upgrade for $5. Hair, eyes, nose, mouth, color, titles and shoes are separate wardrobe choices; a title follows the hero's name. First-clear hats stay free. No purchase changes story access, abilities or quest credit.

Set `STORY_PASS_LAUNCH_AT` to an RFC 3339 UTC time only for a planned launch. New direct pass sales end after 56 days; an existing owner may keep earning afterward, and a standard owner may upgrade afterward. Pass sales require all three matching Paddle price IDs, credentials and `DROPINN_PAYMENTS_ENABLED=1`. The client presents the end date, while the server enforces it. The old supporter pack remains restorable for prior buyers. See [payment setup](payments-setup.md) for product variables and checkout operations.

Progress is stored in `story_pass_credits`, populated only from the server's authoritative collection-credit insert and historical room/credit backfill. The account service reads it through a service-role RPC. Paid tiers derive only from completed payment orders, and the wardrobe validates ownership again when heroes are saved. Apply both September 30 migrations before deploying the new account service. Existing rooms, paid legacy cosmetics and saved hero catalog IDs remain compatible.

Refund policy

Last updated September 26, 2026

The DropInn supporter pack is a one-time digital cosmetic purchase sold through Paddle, the merchant of record. It is not a subscription. Review the pack contents and the final checkout amount, including applicable taxes, before paying.

Requesting a refund

For payment questions or a refund request, contact Paddle payment support and include the transaction or receipt reference. Paddle handles refund requests under its refund policy and applicable law. If the pack is missing from your account after payment, contact themainyak@gmail.com with your purchase reference so we can investigate. Do not send card details by email.

What happens to game items

When a purchase is refunded, its paid hats and palettes are removed from the purchasing account. Free and earned items are unaffected. If a checkout fails or is canceled, no paid items are granted.

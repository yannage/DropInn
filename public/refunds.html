Refund policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

The First Tales Story Pass, Super Supporter pass, upgrade and earlier supporter pack are one-time digital cosmetic purchases sold through Paddle, the merchant of record. They are not subscriptions. Review the contents and final checkout amount, including applicable taxes, before paying.

Requesting a refund

For payment questions or a refund request, contact Paddle payment support and include the transaction or receipt reference. Paddle handles refund requests under its refund policy and applicable law. If purchased cosmetics are missing from your account after payment, contact themainyak@gmail.com with your purchase reference so we can investigate. Do not send card details by email.

What happens to game items

When a purchase is refunded, its paid cosmetic access is removed from the purchasing account. Refunding a standard pass also removes cosmetics granted by its upgrade. Verified quest points and free story hats remain, but pass-track cosmetics require an active pass purchase. If checkout fails or is canceled, no paid items are granted.

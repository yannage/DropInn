Privacy policy

Last updated September 26, 2026

YAK Studios LLC operates DropInn at playdropinn.com. This policy explains the information used to run the game and optional supporter purchases. Questions or data requests can be sent to themainyak@gmail.com.

Information we use

You can play as a guest. If you save an account, we use your sign-in identity, including an email address when supplied by Google or email sign-in. We store your saved heroes, collection, game progress, room participation, chat messages, and reports you submit. We also process technical request information needed to secure and operate the service.

For a supporter purchase, we store the account, order and transaction references, purchase status, price or discount information, and the cosmetics granted to your account. Paddle handles checkout and payment details. DropInn does not receive or store your full card number.

How we use and share it

We use this information to provide multiplayer play, save your progress, deliver purchases, prevent abuse, respond to reports and support requests, and maintain the service. Other players at your table can see your chosen hero and messages you share there.

Our hosting and database providers process data for the service. Paddle processes purchase and refund information as merchant of record. If optional AI narration is enabled, relevant story context or an idea you submit may be sent to the configured AI provider to create game text. Payment card information is not part of that narration request.

Storage and your choices

Your browser stores some game and account recovery settings locally so you can return to a table or resume a purchase. You can clear browser storage, but that may remove local recovery information. Service records are kept as needed to operate accounts, resolve purchases and disputes, protect the game, and meet applicable obligations. To request access, correction, or deletion of your account data, email themainyak@gmail.com. Some purchase and security records may need to be retained.

We may update this policy when the service changes. The date above will show the latest version.

# Drop Inn embroidery edition — v1

Created September 27, 2026. A second shirt, alongside the existing Ivory DTG tee.

## Artwork and provenance

This is a vector adaptation of `../drop-inn-logo-v1/`, retaining the seven original outlined letter paths from `source/lettering-paths.json`. Broader sign shapes replace fine outlines, small pins, rays, and the website plaque. The open doorway and crooked beam remain. No new AI image generation or fonts were used for this adaptation.

- `drop-inn-embroidery.svg`: editable, path-based master; no embedded raster or live text lettering.
- `drop-inn-embroidery-4in-300ppi.png`: transparent sRGB upload, 1200 × 869 pixels at 300 PPI.
- `preview-on-black.png`: artwork preview only.
- `build.mjs`: reproduces the master, PNG, and web-sized official mockups with sharp. Set `SHARP_MODULE` to a local sharp installation if it is not in Node's module path.
- `mockups/`: unmodified 2000 × 2000 PNG exports from Printful's saved product, Flat front/back and Zoomed in front. App WebPs are 1000 × 1000 at quality 86.
- `proof/checkout-entry.png`: public contact/address step, correct Black / M item at $36; no customer details submitted.

Palette: White 1801 `#FFFFFF`, Black 1800 `#000000`, Gold 1951 `#FFCC00`, Kelly Green 1751 `#01784E`. Printful's automatic mapping was inspected and matched all four. Do not use gradients, transparency within filled shapes, fine outlines, or the website text for this placement. Preserve the proportions and surrounding garment space.

The selected Comfort Colors 1717 left-chest template is 4 × 4 inches for all sizes. Artwork was applied at 4.00 × 2.90 inches with standard flat embroidery. Small strap outlines are 20 master units, approximately 0.069 inches at this width. Lettering exceeds the 0.25-inch minimum height. The mockup close-up was visually checked for spelling and clear counters.

This artwork is **not a digitized stitch file or physical sample approval**. Printful digitizes embroidery for production after an order. Its digitizer may adjust stitches or details. Review any production approval request before fulfillment. Official references: [embroidery artwork guidance](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/50263985175953-How-should-I-prepare-my-design-for-embroidery), [digitization process](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/50264003645073-What-is-embroidery-digitization), and [fees](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/50263327450257-What-are-digitization-and-adjustment-fees-for-embroidery).

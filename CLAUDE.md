# DropInn — drop-in tabletop adventures

DropInn’s default experience is V2: discover a live table, join with a ready hero, contribute to a short fantasy adventure, and leave whenever needed. A fresh lobby selects Avalon: A Visit to the Larch Hills v2 (Dice & teamwork); a remembered story selection or invitation keeps its choice, including Avalon v1. Avalon adds a saved seeded episode over six fixed places, two concurrent quest threads and explicit promises. Its v2 edition adds selective D6 attribute checks and shared failed-attempt setups. Mosswater remains a sequential cooperative quest crawl: two exploration actions per focus, six connected places, shared discoveries, separate turn combat, run builds and three different resolutions. Gemward v3 and the earlier adventures remain available with their own pinned rules. The older battle/story prototype remains at `/?legacy=1`; it is not the main product flow.

## Invariants

- The server validates identity, character ownership, actions and room revisions. Browsers send commands, not authoritative room snapshots. Presence updates are separate from gameplay writes.
- A room has four seats, including labeled rules-based companions. New humans replace companions at a turn boundary. Character identity is pinned for the adventure.
- Adventure ID/version are pinned too. Missing identity means legacy Briar Glen v1; unavailable versions fail explicitly. Match only the same adventure/version. Keep released versions addressable when revising definitions.
- Current definitions are Avalon v1, Mosswater v1, Gemward v3, Briar Glen v4 and the other three stories' v2. Gemward v1/v2 remain addressable and never migrate in place. The earlier four stories retain eleven authored preparation, rescue or banking choices and Briar's v3 river supplies. Shared choices resolve against the turn's initial state after all human actions. Guaranteed moves replace ordinary class Help, timing and approaches; companions cannot change choice state. Preserve released versions, branch votes, receipts and rewards. See the [chapter choice supplement](docs/stories/chapter-choices.md).
- Avalon pins its world, generator and content versions plus the resolved manifest. Never reroll a saved cast or established cause. Follow-thread and return commands use the same exact receipt/retry contract as other quest moves. Up to two active threads advance at most three pressure steps, only after meaningful exploration cycles; inspection, absence and combat necessity do not express interest. A third step spends at most one supply once. Return is explicit at Larch Inn after a resolved thread, with unfinished leads and promises retained in the ending.
- Avalon and Mosswater own their sequential lifecycle while sharing the receipt/revision transaction. Inspect freely, prepare locally, confirm one quest action. A hero has two exploration actions under one 45-second deadline; each accepted action shows a 3-second consequence before continuing/passing. Battles pause that focus and give each human one 25-second move before the frozen enemy strike. Only the active hero accrues missed turns; absent Defend grants no mana, XP or companion support. Six combat exchanges end in a costly escape. Shared facts, items, routes, HP and personal run builds survive departure/rejoin. Build/loot commands work off-turn without changing focus, and preserve their exact retry payload across another hero's turn. Checks reveal honest odds and fail forward on essential leads. Endings are explicit; no chapter automatically moves the party. See the [Mosswater packet](docs/stories/mosswater-v1.md).
- Gemward v3 completes town/finale work through recorded preparations and explicit ordinary-turn Help intentions. Gather requires an earlier-turn lead; Finish requires earlier-turn light and neighbour preparations. Every accepted action settles before movement. Six active town rounds or four active finale rounds provide announced fallback; absent-only rounds do not count. Spark dust adds one nonstacking opportunity per area. Watcher knowledge adds one starting battle progress; Oren's packed lantern prevents one damage from the first penetrating strike; the v3 hill road adds one announced strike damage. Repeated known facts never claim another discovery. The final outcome preserves the voted cost and records specific prepared or missing community work. These fields live in the existing authoritative JSON snapshot. See the [v3 packet](docs/stories/gemward-v3.md).
- Gemward browsing is local; release commits a topic or item use. Its saved seed, discoveries, battle, personal stashes/offers and finale live in room JSON. New clues/rewards cannot be spent within their acquisition batch; all accepted exploration actions finish before battle starts. V2 records quest acquisition sources, spends, unlocked edges and confirmed transitions. Its two independent 30-second travel phases freeze available edges and electorate after the chapter reveal: a unique plurality wins, ties/no votes use road then Lantern square/release. Travel grants no action XP or missed-turn penalty. The final vote records the choice; only finale completion consumes the prism or enacts restoration/release. Companions never vote; accepted votes survive departure and exact retries. Pinned v1 retains embedded route preference and first-finale-turn Help/Restore versus Influence/Release. See [expedition rules](docs/expedition-adventures.md).
- Gemward uses guaranteed exploration intentions and a separate two-to-four-round counter encounter instead of the earlier stories' focused approaches. Class signatures and Help differ; progress scales by human count, strongest protection wins, and downed Help protects. Each player holds at most three consumables; full rewards become persistent replace/decline offers. Quest discoveries belong to the room and survive their discoverer's departure. The earlier story library's roll, branch-directed Help and contribution-XP details below remain scoped to those adventures.
- Gemward v3 shows accepted human moves as counters and read-only intentions; pending retries never masquerade as accepted moves. Combat forecasts share the reducer's pure class-move calculation and describe contributions, not a guaranteed final battle result. Overlap advice does not spend or change a move; concurrent redundant Dust prevents release. Optional coaster play is local and closes at the round boundary, returning focus to the story. It sends no gameplay commands and grants no rewards. Fact stamps use confirmed playback timestamps; reconnects and polling never invent an entrance animation.
- River supplies resolve from the turn's initial state: all saved beats partial salvage, which beats a spill. Resolve human moves before loss at river round 3 or early crossing. Guaranteed cargo Help adds no crossing progress. Cargo carries +3/+1 chapel progress or +2 danger once. See the [v3 packet](docs/stories/briar-glen-v3.md).
- In the earlier story library, chapter 3's first turn resolves branch-directed human Help votes together. Unique most votes wins; ties/no votes use the displayed authored fallback. Companions cannot vote. Committed votes survive departure, timing never changes preference, and the chosen cost cannot be reversed by later actions. Gemward v2/v3 use the separate travel contract above.
- Turns allow 60 seconds, resolve early when all active humans commit, and show a paced result sequence for up to ten seconds. Every seated human can skip; if all skip, the next choosing turn starts early. The timeout always advances the table even if someone disconnects.
- New rooms pin `mechanicsVersion: 1`. An optional validated `action.approach` selects focused mechanics; omitted approaches retain standard action behavior, and old rooms retain their original rules. Enemy-targeted Fight can use Quick (+2, 2 progress), Heavy (−1, 6 progress), or Guarded (2 progress, 3 cover). Both sides roll d20; the player must strictly beat the frozen enemy modifier plus its shared turn roll. Progress divides by human count. No approach rerolls either die. Study grants nonstacking +2 next-turn insight; Distract grants +1 opening. Mend is guaranteed 2/3 healing with no progress; retain an accepted Mend's departing recipient until resolution. These optional fields live in existing room/event JSON.
- Normal adventure play keeps four scene targets and the heroes on the stage through selection, waiting and resolution. Use truthful illustrations or clear labeled objects; unrelated props must not imply a state change. All four tokens are visible by default. Dragging prepares; the release control commits. Keep target inspection, dock approaches, tap/keyboard alternatives and 44px controls. Verify 390×844, 320×568 and enlarged text.
- Player guidance derives from the authoritative room, local selection and pending command. It must distinguish choosing, admission, commitment, uncertain delivery, reveal and completion without extending deadlines or preparing a move automatically. First-move teaching is dismissible, replayable and remembered only in this browser; its suggestions never recommend a branch over its alternative. Personal consequences come from recorded round results.
- Party plans describe accepted human commands, never drafts or resolved outcomes. Scene approach names stay visible; route votes remain independent of contribution-roll odds. `actionOdds` enumerates possible d20 outcomes using the current traits, support and approach; it never reads seeded rolls. Chapter choice credit records successful contributors and original setup sources in optional JSON metadata, retains their names after departure, and adds no XP or numerical benefit.
- **Show the consequence on the table before explaining it in history.** Only confirmed events animate through `stagePlayback.ts`; cache the last choosing presentation without changing authoritative state. Timestamp-based beats finish by 5.75 seconds, late snapshots fast-forward, and reloads without a cache show the resolved board. Gemward v2/v3 keep ordinary play on the table with on-demand Chronicle; Journey opens at explicit travel boundaries. V3 keeps the last accepted consequence readable between turns, after its live scene reveal. Earlier stories retain delayed automatic round parchment and per-round View scene dismissal. Preserve exact pending-command retries, the ten-second reveal, readiness behavior, manual reading and one narrator. Effects stay bounded, muted preferences persist, and reduced motion shows complete outcomes immediately.
- Assist remains the wire token `assist`, displayed as **Help**. In combat, Help on the announced victim (`targetKind: 'hero'`) is Protect: guaranteed 2 protection, or 3 with a good release; no objective progress and 3 contribution XP. Strongest Protect and existing party cover win rather than stack. Existing absent-player defense remains separate. Downed heroes can Protect.
- Enemy intent freezes source, intended actor and base damage at the choosing boundary. Prefer upright humans, then upright companions. Retain a departing threatened seat through resolution without redirecting its strike; an empty uncommitted room releases seats and parks. Old snapshots without intent finish the current turn under their existing targeting rule; the next choosing boundary announces intent.
- Release input is an optional whole number from 0–1200 ms. Inclusive 650–950 ms grants +1 to the server-computed modifier, or the extra Protect point. Missing/missed timing gives the ordinary move. Assisted release has the same cap; Roll now skips timing. Timing is client input, not proof of human dexterity or an anti-cheat mechanism. The server deadline still applies, including signed Spotlight confirmation.
- Structured `StoryEvent.result` fields drive progress, target-change, protection, healing and damage visuals. `contribution: true` marks real actions including Protect without inventing a roll. Reward/recap/metrics readers also recognize old events with a roll. Room snapshots and event entries are already JSON; these optional fields require no SQL migration.
- Missing input abstains in social scenes and defends in combat. Two missed turns release the seat. Empty rooms finish committed work and park; companions do not generate unattended progress.
- Current turn identifiers reject stale actions. Same-turn submissions may use older revisions; the server retries transaction conflicts against fresh state. Command receipts and reward deltas are idempotent.
- Uncertain moves persist their complete action, release timing and command ID with the local saved table. Freeze edits until retry or synchronization settles the command. Reload must not manufacture a new timing attempt or duplicate rewards.
- Each hero has one Spotlight attempt per chapter. Leaving and returning preserves its use, HP and contributions. Validated previews are signed and bound to the user, room, target and turn.
- Class presets determine starting power regardless of saved XP. Downed heroes can Help. Progress and danger contributions scale with human count; every chapter closes by ten rounds.
- New tables pin `collectionVersion: 1`; the four First tales stories grant one Thread per contributed chapter on every outcome. Gemward awards XP and keepsakes but is not part of that Thread/Story Pass catalog. Account-wide collection ownership derives from the server ledger and exact keepsake strings; hero inventories stay personal. Crafting spends/grants atomically with a durable command ID, never auto-equips, and appearance stays pinned during a visit. Old rooms retain their reward policy. See [collection rollout and verification](docs/collections.md).
- First Tales Story Pass credit comes from verified contribution to completed chapters, with first-clear story hats free and paid track cosmetics derived from the separate pass ledger. Purchases never grant quest progress. Refunds remove paid items, while earned progress and free hats remain. Keep prices and sale dates server-controlled; see [Story Pass rollout](docs/story-pass.md).
- Authored mechanics own outcomes. Optional AI can prepare cosmetic variations, interpret supported scene interactions, and narrate resolved events. It cannot grant arbitrary rewards or revise a resolved turn.

## Development and deployment

`npm run dev` provides both Vite and the local command service. The local repository is server memory: rooms and chat disappear when the development server restarts; browser hero data remains. Use `?session=host` and `?session=guest` for separate local identities.

`npm run test:story-table`, `npm run test:journey` and `npm run test:expedition` run `scripts/playtest-story-table.mjs` for current Gemward v3. `npm run test:journey:v2` retains the pinned v2 journey runner. Pass a loopback origin as a positional argument. Keep each version's evidence separate.

`npm run test:quest -- http://127.0.0.1:5207` runs the maintained Mosswater two-player browser journey against an isolated local handler, including real retries, follow-ups, map suggestions, all combat moves, run upgrades, loot and a complete ending at phone/desktop sizes. `questRun.test.ts`, `server/questRun.test.ts` and `adventureStore.quest.test.ts` cover all three resolutions, seeded routes, admission, absence, receipts and recovery. Browser evidence is local, not hosted persistence or Realtime proof.

`npm run test:avalon -- http://127.0.0.1:5209` runs the saved-world, leads, promises and explicit-return two-player journey with the real isolated local handler. `avalonContent.test.ts`, `avalon.test.ts` and `server/avalon.test.ts` cover the immutable manifest, all nine solutions, capped pressure/supplies, shared promise consumption, parked participants and exact receipts. `test:story-selection` compares Avalon, Mosswater and both newer Gemward editions without changing their pins.

`npm run test:flipbooks` checks real loading, eight raster frames, reduced motion, hidden/late playback and failed-image recovery against local Vite on port 5204. Override the loopback origin with `FLIPBOOK_TEST_URL`. Confirmed discovery/contact playback is covered by `test:journey`.

**Restart Vite after changing the server handler or its server-side dependencies.** The development plugin caches the loaded handler. `npm run preview` serves static assets only and does not provide `/api/dropinn`.

Hosted multiplayer requires Supabase Auth, migrations, browser Supabase configuration, and server-only credentials in the Netlify function. Never place service-role, signing, or model API secrets in `VITE_*` variables. See [service setup](server/DROPINN.md) and [Supabase setup](SUPABASE_SETUP.md).

The new scene-stage mechanics still need a separately authorized hosted rollout. Earlier hosted API checks do not verify this working-tree version, browser Realtime, or physical-phone play. Actual model evaluation and friend-group playtesting remain follow-ups. [NEXT.md](NEXT.md) records verification boundaries; [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md) describes the product behavior.
